# Task decomposition for blocked work

Use this pattern when a person understands the goal but cannot identify a safe first action:

> I am stuck starting [task]. Break it into steps that each take less than one minute. Give me only the first step, tell me what to open or touch, and wait for my confirmation before giving the next step. Ask one clarifying question if the task is ambiguous. Do not send, purchase, delete, publish, or change account/security settings.

## Why this belongs in adoption training

The useful capability is progressive disclosure: reduce cognitive load without handing the entire task to an agent. The person remains the decision-maker and confirms progress.

## Boundaries

- Do not include passwords, API keys, private legal facts, health details, or confidential client information in the prompt.
- The prompt is a productivity aid, not a diagnosis or treatment for ADHD, anxiety, depression, or another condition.
- For financial, legal, medical, security, or irreversible tasks, pause for qualified review or explicit human approval.
- If the first step reveals a risk, stop and reassess rather than continuing mechanically.

## Evaluation

A good response gives one concrete, reversible action. It does not invent missing context, produce a giant checklist, or perform an external side effect without approval.
