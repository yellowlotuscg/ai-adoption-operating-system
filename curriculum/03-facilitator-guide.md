# Facilitator guide: check before you trust

This is a proposed 60-minute workshop, not a record of a delivered client session. Use synthetic examples only. No paid model or vendor account is required: learners can assess the supplied draft on paper.

## Preparation

Read [the exercise](04-source-checking-lab.md) and [assessment](../templates/assessment.md). Confirm the facilitator, learner, reviewer and approved tool/workspace. Do not collect employee performance or client records for this public example. Explain that no message will be sent.

## Session

- Minutes 0-10: classify public, internal and confidential inputs. Ask who approves a tool and who can approve an external action.
- Minutes 10-20: introduce the source packet. Learners mark each draft sentence supported, contradicted or unknown.
- Minutes 20-35: rewrite the draft, citing the relevant source ID beside each factual claim. Leave missing facts unresolved.
- Minutes 35-45: pairs review using the assessment. Ask whether the draft is safe to send and who must approve it.
- Minutes 45-55: practice one role variation below. Use only the packet, not a real customer record.
- Minutes 55-60: choose one small pilot and complete its stop condition. Record feedback without personal identifiers.

## Role variations

Customer service: prepare a reply about the delayed order, without promising a refund.
Operations: prepare an internal exception note with missing information and the next human decision.
Manager: assess the draft and decide whether it is ready for review, not automatically ready to send.

## Debrief

Ask what the model invented, what could not be verified and what stayed with the human. A confident tone is not evidence. If a learner misses the refund or sensitive-data issue, repeat that portion before any pilot.

## Deployment handoff

Pair with the [Deployment Starter training guide](https://github.com/yellowlotuscg/small-business-deployment-starter/blob/main/docs/ai-training-guide.md). Configure approved data handling and named reviewers before using real business inputs. This workshop grants no permission to send, spend, change accounts or deploy.
