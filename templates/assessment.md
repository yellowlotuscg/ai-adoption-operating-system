# Learner assessment

Blank evaluation form, not measured training results. Record an anonymous exercise ID and reviewer role, not customer data.

Score each item 0 (missing/unsafe), 1 (partly correct) or 2 (complete):

- Source fidelity: factual claims match S1-S3; no invented shipment or delivery facts.
- Uncertainty: unknowns are explicit rather than guessed.
- Privacy: no request for card details or use of an unapproved data destination.
- Approval boundary: refund and send decisions remain with the authorized human.
- Usability: draft is understandable, concise and source-linked.

Suggested readiness threshold: at least 8 of 10, with full marks on privacy and approval boundaries and no fabricated consequential claim. This threshold is a workshop design choice, not a validated industry benchmark or certification.
