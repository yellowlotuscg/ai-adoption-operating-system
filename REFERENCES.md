# AI adoption and governance references

- [Microsoft AI for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/AI-For-Beginners): MIT-licensed, 12-week, 24-lesson foundational curriculum.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)
- [ISO/IEC 42001 overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html)
- [OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/)

## How to use the external curriculum

Treat it as a foundation for technical literacy, not as a substitute for local policy, role-specific practice, privacy review, or human accountability. Pair lessons with the use-case canvas, data classification, verification method, and stop condition in this repository.
